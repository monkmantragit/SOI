ACL Surgery Recovery Timeline (India)

After ACL reconstruction, most patients walk within a few days, return to desk work in 1-2 weeks, jog by 3-4 months, and return to sport at 9-12 months - provided they complete a structured physiotherapy programme.

Week 0-2: protect and reduce swelling

Focus on controlling swelling, regaining full knee extension, and activating the quadriceps. You will walk with crutches and a brace as advised.

Weeks 2-6: restore movement

Range of motion improves toward a full bend, gait normalises, and you wean off crutches. Most desk workers are back at work.

Months 2-4: build strength

Strengthening progresses; light straight-line jogging often begins around 3-4 months once strength targets are met.

Months 4-9: sport-specific training

Agility, cutting and sport drills are added gradually, guided by objective strength and control tests.

Months 9-12: return to sport

Return to pivoting sport is cleared only after passing functional tests - rushing this stage is the main cause of re-injury. See our ACL reconstruction in Bangalore page, and consult Dr Naveen Kumar L V.

Frequently asked questions

How long is ACL surgery recovery?

Most people walk within days, return to desk work in 1-2 weeks, and return to sport at 9-12 months after a structured rehabilitation programme.

When can I drive after ACL surgery?

Usually 4-6 weeks for a right-knee surgery once you have good control, sooner for a left knee with an automatic car - always confirm with your surgeon.

When can I return to running?

Light straight-line jogging is typically allowed around 3-4 months, once strength and control targets are met.

What slows down ACL recovery?

Skipping physiotherapy, returning to sport too early, and poor quadriceps strength are the biggest causes of delay or re-injury.
