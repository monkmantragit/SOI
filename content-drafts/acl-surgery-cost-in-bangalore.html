What determines the cost of ACL surgery?

Four things drive the price: the graft, the fixation implants, the anaesthesia and theatre time, and the length of hospital stay. Because ACL reconstruction is usually a day-care or single-night procedure, costs are more predictable than for major joint replacement.

Is ACL surgery covered by insurance?

Yes - when it is medically indicated, ACL reconstruction is covered by most Indian health-insurance policies and cashless TPAs. Our front desk assists with pre-authorisation.
