Repair or trim?

Preserving the meniscus with a repair protects the knee cartilage over the long term and is preferred where the tear pattern allows. A trimming is used for tears that cannot be repaired. Read more about managing a meniscus tear.

What's included

The estimate usually includes the surgeon and anaesthesia fees, theatre, day-care or single-night stay and the first follow-up. Post-operative physiotherapy is billed separately.

Meniscus repair vs meniscectomy - which is cheaper?

A meniscectomy is sometimes slightly less expensive than a repair, but preserving the meniscus with a repair usually protects the knee better long term. The right choice depends on the tear pattern.

Is meniscus surgery covered by insurance?

Yes, arthroscopic meniscus surgery is covered by most Indian health-insurance policies when medically indicated.

How soon can I walk after meniscus surgery?

Most patients walk with support the same or next day. Full recovery ranges from a few weeks (trim) to a few months (repair).
