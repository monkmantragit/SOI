How long is recovery after rotator cuff surgery?

You will usually wear a sling for a few weeks, followed by structured physiotherapy. Most people regain useful function by 3-6 months.

Can a rotator cuff tear heal without surgery?

Small or partial tears can improve with physiotherapy and injections. Larger tears usually need arthroscopic repair for the best outcome.
